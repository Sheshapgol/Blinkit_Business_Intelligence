# **Blinkit Business Intelligence Project**



### **Project Overview**



This project focused on analysing Blinkit's business data to gain an understanding of how different areas of their business were performing. I used multiple data sets relating to customers, orders, products, deliveries, inventory \& marketing to gain a better insight into the business.



The aim of this project was to understand more than just the raw data and look for valuable insights within the data. I used Python to analyse and explore the data sets, I used MySQL to query the data using SQL \& answer business questions and I used Power BI to create interactive dashboards.



I looked at trends in sales, customer activity, product performance, delivery efficiency, inventory levels \& marketing performance. This analysis has helped me to identify key trends and understand how data can be used to help make better business decisions.

#### **2. Problem Statement**

#### 

Blinkit is a company that creates business data through various activities like Customer Orders, Product Sales, Deliveries, Inventory and Marketing Campaigns. Analyzing the data from each activity individually can make it hard to get an overall view of how your business is performing and where you may want to focus your efforts.



This project was created to collate the data from each source and analyze what it tells us about the business. Using Python, SQL and Power BI, I was able to analyze key business metrics and identify trends in the data.



This project focuses on extracting insights from our raw business data to help us better understand how the business is performing.



#### **3. Project Objectives**



* Explore various data sources associated with Blinkit.
* Understand the sales patterns of the company, customer behaviour and product trends.
* Analyse delivery patterns and investigate potential causes for delays.
* Investigate inventory data to get an understanding of stock availability.
* Analyse the effectiveness of marketing campaigns using key business metrics.
* Create interactive dashboards in Power BI and provide business insights.

#### **Key Business Insights**



The analysis provided several useful insights into Blinkit's business performance.



* Sales Performance: The dataset recorded total sales of ₹1,10,09,308.50 across 5,000 orders, with an average order value of ₹2,201.86.





* Delivery Performance: Out of 5,000 orders, 3,470 were delivered on time, representing 69.4% of total orders.





* Payment Methods: Customers used different payment options, including Card, Cash, Wallet, and UPI, with relatively balanced order counts.



* Marketing Performance: The marketing analysis recorded an average Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) of 2.74.



* Business Analysis: Python, SQL, and Power BI helped examine different areas of the business and present the findings in an understandable format.



These findings helped me better understand Blinkit's sales, delivery operations, customer payment preferences, and marketing performance.
